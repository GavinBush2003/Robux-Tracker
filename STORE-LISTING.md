# Chrome Web Store listing

## Summary
See where your Robux went: spending per game, Robux bought, earnings, and your balance.

## Description
See where your Robux went.

Robux Tracker reads your own Roblox transaction history and adds it up. It shows what you spent in each game, what you bought, how much Robux you bought and earned, and how much real money you paid.

What it does:
- Totals for every game you've spent in, with the items you bought
- Your Robux balance, what came in, and what went out
- Robux you bought, with the real price of each pack
- Your earnings from sales, commissions and payouts
- A full list of every transaction
- Works with more than one account, so you can add your main and alts together
- Export to CSV or JSON

It shows up in the left sidebar on roblox.com.

Everything stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded and there's no account or tracking. It only reads your history. It never buys or changes anything.
